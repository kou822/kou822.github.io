Frequently asked questions

How do I add a friend?

One of you creates an invite code and the other enters it (or scans the QR code). No account is needed.

Who can see my location?

Only a friend you added by invitation, only when they use "Find", and only according to your setting (Off / While app is open / Anytime). Location is encrypted between devices; the operator's server cannot read it and does not store it.

I don't get a notification when someone looks for me

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Beecon. To answer with "Anytime", location access must be set to "Always".

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Settings → Privacy → "Delete all data" erases your friendships, keys, display name and notification history, and tells your friends that the friendship has ended.
