Beecon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 ・ 日本語

This is an English translation of the Japanese policy. If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

This policy explains how Koichiro Yamashita ("we") handles user information in the iOS app "Beecon" ("the app"). The app helps you find a friend you have lost inside a building by sharing location only with people you have approved in advance.

1. Information collected and why

Type of informationPurpose
Location (precise location from GPS and similar sources) To deliver your location only to the devices of friends you have approved when you are separated, and to show the distance, direction and relative height to that friend. Location is end-to-end encrypted between devices; our server cannot read it and does not store it. Distance and similar values are calculated on the device.
Sensor information (Bluetooth signal strength, UWB (nearby ranging), barometric pressure and motion) To estimate the distance, direction and floor (height) of a nearby friend more accurately. This is processed mainly on the device.
Push notification token Kept on the server as a delivery address so that push notifications such as "someone is looking for you" can reach you. The app has no accounts or user IDs, and does not collect your name or email address.
Information related to push delivery (collected by Google): Firebase installation ID, delivery measurement data, and diagnostic information about how the SDK operates (technical information such as errors and performance) To deliver push notifications, measure their delivery, and maintain and improve Firebase services. Google collects this directly through the Firebase SDK and handles it under Google's policies. It is needed for push notifications whether or not ads are shown. It does not include your location, your friendships or your display name.
Display name (optional) So that friends can recognize you. It is stored on your device and delivered directly to your friends' devices in encrypted form (the server cannot read it). Setting one is optional.
Advertising-related information (collected by Google): device identifiers (scoped to the app or developer), IP address and the approximate region inferred from it, ads shown, interaction data such as app launches and taps, crash logs, and performance data such as launch time, hang rate and energy use To show ads in the app (Google AdMob) and to measure delivery, prevent fraud and improve the service. Google collects this directly through the ads SDK and handles it under Google's policies. The app requests non-personalized ads, but that setting only affects how ads are chosen; it does not stop the collection described above. Your location (GPS), your friendships and your display name are never used for advertising or passed to Google. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for it before showing ads (you can change it in the app under Settings → Privacy).

2. Who your location is shared with

Your location is shared with another user only when a friend you approved in advance (by accepting an invitation) uses "Find" to look for you, and you have allowed sharing, either at that moment or through your settings. Your location never reaches anyone you have not approved. You can change whether you can be found at any time with the in-app "Location sharing" setting (Off / While app is open / Anytime). That setting is stored only on your device, and the device itself makes the decision. Your friendships are also stored only on your device; our server does not know who is friends with whom.

3. Third parties and external services

The app uses the following external services. Each company handles data under its own policies.

  • Google Firebase (push notification delivery) — Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob (in-app ad delivery and measurement) — Google's advertising policies

Other than the above, we do not provide or sell your information to third parties, except where required by law.

4. Storage and deletion

Friendships, encryption keys, your display name, sharing settings and notification history are stored only on your device. You can erase them at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → "Delete all data", which also tells your friends that the friendship has ended.

Deleting (uninstalling) the app ends your friendships. Because of how the device works, some items such as encryption keys may remain in a protected area of the device, but the app erases them first when it is installed again, so earlier friendships and sharing settings do not come back. They are not carried over by restoring from a backup or moving to a new device either. Notification history keeps only the latest 50 entries, for 30 days.

The server keeps only the following.

What is keptFor how long
Encrypted messages such as locations and search requests (in memory only; we cannot read the contents) Until the recipient receives them, for at most 30 seconds (within about 1 minute at the latest, including the interval of the periodic cleanup)
Encrypted messages announcing changes to a display name or sharing setting, or the end of a friendship (in memory only) Until the recipient receives them, for at most 7 days
Notification delivery addresses (the push notification token and a hash of the address from which the contents cannot be guessed) 180 days after last use. The address is deleted when the friendship ends
Hashes of ended addresses (a record to prevent reuse)90 days
Invite codes (public keys and encrypted information)Until the friend has been added, for at most 24 hours

The server does not keep a history of locations and does not write encrypted messages to disk.

5. Children's privacy

The app has no age restriction. There is no account registration, and the app does not ask for or collect a user's age, name or contact details. The information collected is only what this policy describes, regardless of age. If a child uses the app, we ask a parent or guardian to review together who the child shares their location with.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when necessary. Important changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
